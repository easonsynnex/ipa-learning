Diphthong audio: ei.wav, ai.wav, oi.wav, au.wav, oh.wav, ia.wav, ea.wav, ua.wav

These files contain one complete pronunciation cropped from the corresponding
sound_*.mp3 files in Medo-king-01/english-ipa-chart. The source MP3 files match
the public audio hosted by IPAChart.app. The files were trimmed to remove a
second pronunciation and surrounding silence; they were not assembled from
separate vowel recordings.

Source: https://github.com/Medo-king-01/english-ipa-chart
Original audio: https://ipachart.app/
